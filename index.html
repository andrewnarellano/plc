League Values & Expectations
Ease of
Management
The weekly roster management for each owner and the overall management of the league should require minimal time. The desire of the league is for minimal oversight and maximum freedom. Only when necessary, the Leadership Team will step in to correct an issue.
Involved
Owners
The ideal team owner will be responsive to communications, contribute to the culture of the league, and courteous in trade discussions with other owners. The league charges a league fee each year and provides cash awards as an incentive to each owner to stay involved.
Fair Play
The primary duty of the Leadership Team is to guarantee that fair play is promoted. This includes making rules and owner adjustments as necessary. Each member of the league is responsible to manage their team in a way that insures the integrity of the League.
Mirror
Real Life
The scoring structure should be as close to real football as possible. All categories are assessed on a yearly basis to keep the League current with trends in the NFL. Scoring adjustments are made with care by the Leadership Team.
